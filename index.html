About

I'm a software engineer with 6+ years of experience building large-scale financial security systems at MG Community Credit Cooperatives and Samsung SDS — specializing in biometric authentication, mobile OTP, and interbank network infrastructure.

Through hands-on work developing authentication systems, I've grown deeply curious about the human layer of security: why do technically sound systems so often fail in real-world use? This question drives my interest in Usable Security and Human-Computer Interaction.

I'm now pursuing graduate study in the U.S. to explore how we can design security systems that are both robust and genuinely usable — bridging the gap between engineering rigor and human-centered design.

Outside of engineering, I produce music and explore audio technology as a creative practice.
